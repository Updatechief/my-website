Oakline began as a small workshop with one carpenter and a second-hand saw. Today a team of four builds custom furniture from locally sourced hardwood.

How we work

  1. Listen first: we visit your space and talk through how you use it
  2. Design: we sketch the piece and agree the details with you
  3. Build: we make it in our workshop and keep you updated
  4. Deliver: we bring it to your home and assemble it

Our Promise

We use sustainably sourced wood, we explain our prices clearly, and we repair anything that fails within the first year.
